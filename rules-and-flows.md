# Rules and flows — ArchMakers v1

ArchMakers is a directory of 3D printing, laser-cutting and model-making companies ("makers") for architects. Architects search for free and send one quote request to up to 5 makers. Makers list themselves for free and can upgrade to **Pro**. ArchMakers never handles money between architects and makers.

## 2. The three most important flows

### Flow A — Architect finds makers and requests quotes

1. The architect opens ArchMakers and searches by **category** (3D printing, laser cutting or model-making studio) and **city or region**.
2. They filter by scale (e.g. 1:200), material, typical lead time, express option and price level.
3. They open maker pages to see photos of real architecture models, services, location and response rate.
4. They tick **up to 5 makers** and click **Request quotes**.
5. They fill in the form: **3D file** (STL, OBJ or 3MF), **scale**, **material and colour**, **size**, **deadline**, **budget range**, delivery or pickup, and notes.
6. The site runs a **basic file check** (file opens, size and units look right, warns about walls that are too thin at the chosen scale). The architect fixes the file or confirms "send anyway".
7. If they aren't logged in, they enter their email and confirm it with a one-time link. That creates their architect account.
8. The request is sent. Archie shows: "Your request went to 5 makers. Most reply within 48 hours."
9. Each maker gets an email plus the request in their inbox. Replies arrive in the architect's **ArchMakers inbox** and by email.
10. The architect compares the replies and contacts the maker they choose **directly** (payment and delivery happen between them).
11. **7 days later** the architect gets one short email: "Did you order? From whom?" (one click to answer).

### Flow B — Maker claims a listing and replies to requests

1. A maker finds their company page (from our outreach email, Instagram DM or a Google search), marked **"Unclaimed: is this your company?"**
2. They click **Claim this listing** and create an account.
3. They prove it's their company: either an email from the company's own domain, or a code sent to the phone number on their public website or Google listing. If neither works, the admin checks by hand.
4. The listing becomes **claimed (Free)**. They edit their details: categories, scales, materials, lead time, express yes or no, price level, minimum order value, languages, and photos (max 3 on Free).
5. When a matching quote request arrives, they get an email and see it in their inbox.
6. They open the request, download the file, and either:
   - **reply** with a price, delivery date and a short message, or
   - **decline** with one click and a reason ("too busy", "outside my scales", "budget too low").
7. Replying or declining within **48 hours** keeps their response rate high. Silence lowers it.

### Flow C — Maker upgrades to Pro (and cancels)

1. Every newly claimed maker gets **Pro free for 3 months**.
2. Near the end of the trial, the maker sees their stats ("You received 14 requests and 230 page views") and an offer to keep Pro.
3. They choose **monthly** (€29 in Germany / €9 in North Macedonia and Kosovo) or **yearly** (two months free).
4. They pay through the payment provider. Pro is active immediately: top position in its category, unlimited photos and requests, stats, and the Verified badge once its criteria are met.
5. **Cancel:** in account settings, one click on "Cancel Pro". Pro stays active until the end of the paid period, then the listing becomes Free. It isn't deleted.
6. If a payment fails, the maker gets an email and **7 days** to fix it before falling back to Free.

## 3. Business rules

1. **Architects never pay.** Searching, viewing and sending quote requests are always free.
2. **A quote request must include** a 3D file, a scale, a deadline and a budget range. Requests without them can't be sent.
3. **The deadline must be at least 3 working days** after the request. Shorter deadlines are rejected with a message explaining why.
4. **One request goes to at most 5 makers.** An architect can send at most **10 requests per day**, to stop spam.
5. **Makers only receive matching requests**: the request must fit their categories, scales and minimum order value.
6. **Makers must reply or decline within 48 hours.** The response rate shown on their page counts the last 90 days.
7. **Free makers receive their first 3 requests per month.** Later requests that month go to other matching makers, and the Free maker sees "You missed 4 requests. Upgrade to Pro."
8. **"Verified" requires all of:** a claimed listing, an active Pro plan, at least 5 photos of real architecture work, and a response rate of at least 80%. If any of these is lost, the badge disappears automatically.
9. **Unclaimed listings show only public business information** (name, city, website, services). Any company can ask to be removed, and we remove it within **48 hours**.
10. **Uploaded files are confidential:** only the makers the request was sent to can download them. Files are **deleted 60 days** after the request.
11. **No payments through ArchMakers in v1.** Price, payment, delivery and refunds are agreed directly between architect and maker, and ArchMakers isn't part of that contract.
12. **Pro is paid in advance and can be cancelled anytime**, effective at the end of the current period. There are no refunds for a partly used month.
13. **Reviews and complaints:** an architect can report a maker after a reply. A maker with **3 confirmed complaints in 6 months** loses the Verified badge, and the admin can hide the listing.
14. **Makers may not ask architects to pay outside their own business** (for example, fake deposits). Breaking this rule removes the maker immediately.

## 4. What version 1 will NOT do

- ❌ **No payments between architects and makers.** No checkout, no commission on orders, no refunds through ArchMakers.
- ❌ **No deadline or quality guarantee.** We show response rates and photos, but the contract is between architect and maker.
- ❌ **No instant prices.** Makers reply with their own quotes.
- ❌ **No automatic file repair.** We only warn about problems.
- ❌ **No construction 3D printing** (printing real buildings). That's a different customer, for later.
- ❌ **No maker listings in North Macedonia and Kosovo yet.** Germany first, Balkans in phase 2.
- ❌ **No public star reviews** (only complaints to the admin in v1).
- ❌ **No AI assistant** that writes the request for you.
- ❌ **No mobile app** (the website works on phones).
- ❌ **No Viber/WhatsApp/SMS notifications** (email only).
- ❌ **No languages other than German and English.**
