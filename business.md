# ArchMakers — Business (parts 1–4)

**Pitch:** ArchMakers is a website where architects find 3D printing, laser-cutting and model-making companies that do architecture work, and request quotes from up to five of them with one form.

**Idea origin:** the idea came from my cousin, an architect in Germany, who described the need for one place to find model makers and reach several at once.

## 1. The problem

**Problem sentence** *(draft, to confirm with the real interviews)*:
Architects in Germany who need a physical model for a competition, jury or client meeting spend 3–7 days finding a maker who does architecture work and waiting for quotes, which often means paying for express service, risking poor quality, or being stressed right up to the deadline.

## 2. The big picture

**In one sentence:** ArchMakers is a website where architects find 3D printing, laser-cutting and model-making companies that do architecture work, and request quotes from up to five of them with one form.

**Who uses it**
- Architects and small studios in Germany (models for competitions, juries and client meetings)
- Architecture students (in Germany, and North Macedonia and Kosovo as a test market)
- Makers: 3D print shops, laser-cutting shops and model-making studios

**Who pays**
- **Architects and students pay nothing.**
- **Makers pay** for a **Pro listing** (€29/month in Germany, €9/month in North Macedonia and Kosovo), but only after a free 3-month Pro trial, once they can see the requests they're getting.

**Why now**
- Entry-level 3D printers capable of architecture models are now cheap, so there are many new small makers, but architects can't find the ones who do *architecture* work. Search results mix them with figurine and hobby printers.
- The big platforms (Craftcloud, Xometry) are built for engineering parts, not for scales, facades and jury deadlines.
- Hosting, domains and email cost almost nothing today, so a niche directory can earn money from a few dozen paying makers.

### Four ways to solve the same problem

| | Solution | How it works | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Directory + group quote request** *(chosen)* | Searchable list of makers; one form sends a request to up to 5 | Fast to build, no payments or refunds, can be pre-filled with real companies, makers pay | Architects may contact makers directly next time; needs Google traffic |
| **B** | **Concierge service** | Architect sends the file by email or WhatsApp; I find the maker and keep a margin | Can start tomorrow with no code; I learn exactly what's needed | Doesn't scale; I carry the risk if a model is late |
| **C** | **Full marketplace with payments** | Upload, instant price, pay online, platform guarantees the deadline | Highest value per order, commission on every sale | Hardest to build; payments, refunds and VAT invoices; online payments are hard to set up from North Macedonia |
| **D** | **Own workshop** | Buy printers and a laser cutter and make the models myself | Full control of quality and price | Expensive, needs space and model-making skills, can't cover a whole country |

**Why I chose A:** it matches the need my cousin described, it's the option I can build well and launch quickly, and it avoids handling payments. It also leads naturally to C later: once architects use the directory, I can add "order and pay through ArchMakers" as version 2. In the first weeks I'll also handle some requests by hand (like B) to learn what makers and architects really need.

## 4. Business model and price

### Lean Canvas

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | 1) Days lost finding makers who do architecture work. 2) Getting quotes means emailing 4–5 shops and waiting. 3) Quality is unknown until the model arrives. *Alternatives:* Google, Instagram, Craftcloud, Xometry, colleagues |
| **Customer segments** | Users (free): architects, small studios and architecture students in Germany; MK and XK students as a test market. Payers: 3D print shops, laser cutters and model studios. Early adopters: my cousin's studio and network, small shops posting architecture models on Instagram |
| **Unique value proposition** | Find makers who really build architecture models, and get five quotes with one form, for free |
| **Solution** | Directory filtered by category, scale, material and lead time; one quote request to up to 5 makers; company pages with real model photos and response rate |
| **Channels** | LinkedIn, Instagram, my cousin's network, Google (city and category pages, guides), architecture faculties, direct email |
| **Revenue streams** | Pro listing for makers: €29/month in Germany, €9/month in MK and XK; yearly = 2 months free. Basic listing is free |
| **Cost structure** | Hosting, database and storage, domain, email, my time. About €1–7/month at launch, about €75/month at ~1,000 users |
| **Key metrics** | Quote requests per week; % answered within 48 h; claimed listings; Pro subscribers |
| **Unfair advantage** | Direct access to German architects through my cousin; architecture-only focus; I speak Albanian and Macedonian for Balkan makers |

### Price and why

**Pro: €29/month (Germany), €9/month (MK and XK), monthly, cancel anytime; yearly gets 2 months free. Every new maker gets Pro free for 3 months.**

- **Monthly instead of pay-per-lead:** simple and predictable for makers, with no arguments about "bad leads", and predictable income for me.
- **€29:** less than the profit on one small model order, and under the "ask the boss" limit for small shops.
- **Cheaper in the Balkans:** lower prices and incomes there, and it's a test market.
